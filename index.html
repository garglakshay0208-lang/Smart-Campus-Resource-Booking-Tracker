The Campus Chaos

Campus resources shouldn't be booked in chat.

When student societies and faculty coordinate specialized rooms through unstructured WhatsApp groups and emails, clashes are inevitable.

Double Bookings

Two teams accidentally reserve the same resource at the same hour, ruining workshops and guest presentations.

Hidden Availability

Nobody knows whether the room is actually free or reserved off-the-record, leaving expensive facilities underutilized.

Scattered Communication

Bookings disappear inside WhatsApp and group chats where timestamps are disputed and admins get overwhelmed.

No Central Record

There is no reliable booking history for department heads, audit tracking, or campus equipment accountability.

1. Atomic Lock Acquisition

Every booking executes with a conditional write on DynamoDB. If two users click submit at the exact same millisecond, only the first request succeeds; the second receives a ConditionalCheckFailedException.

2. WebSocket Real-Time Fanout

DynamoDB Streams triggers an AWS Lambda that publishes a mutation message through API Gateway WebSockets, updating all active campus browser timelines within 40ms.
